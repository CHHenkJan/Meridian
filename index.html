The Method

Three signals must align before Meridian acts.

1

Trend Bias

EMA(5) must be above EMA(15) on the daily chart. Short-term momentum must be bullish. No exceptions.

if EMA(5) > EMA(15) → trend = bullish
2

Pullback Entry

Price must pull back to and bounce from the EMA(21). We don't chase — we wait for the market to come to us.

price touches EMA(21) + bounce confirmed → entry zone
3

MACD Confirmation

The MACD histogram must be recovering (turning positive). Momentum must be returning, not fading.

MACD histogram > prev bar → momentum confirmed
✓

All Three Align: Meridian Buys

When any one is absent: Meridian waits. Patience is a feature, not a bug.
