How to Read the Track Record

Every trade in the log includes entry date, pair, entry price, exit price, hold duration, and percentage result. Both winning and losing trades are shown — there is no cherry-picking.

Meridian's methodology produces both winners and losers. The edge comes from the system's ability to cut losses quickly (via ATR trailing stops) while letting winners develop over multiple days.

Importantly: all trades are executed on a paper trading account. No real capital is at risk. This means there is no slippage, no execution delay, and no emotional decision-making. Real trading results may differ significantly.
