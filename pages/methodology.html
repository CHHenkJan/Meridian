The Method

Triple Confirmation Swing Entry

How Meridian finds, enters, and manages swing trades on daily crypto timeframes.

The Philosophy

Scalping requires constant screen time. Long-term investing requires patience that most people simply don't have. Swing trading sits in the middle — and that's exactly where Meridian operates.

Daily timeframes mean you check your positions once a day. Multi-day holds mean fees don't eat your returns. Trend confirmation means you're not fighting the market.

It's the approach that professional systematic traders have used for decades — now accessible to anyone with a Cryptohopper account.

Entry Rules

Three signals must align before Meridian acts.

1

Trend Bias — EMA(5) / EMA(15)

EMA(5) must be above EMA(15) on the daily chart. Short-term momentum must be bullish. No exceptions. This filter keeps Meridian out of bearish or sideways markets where pullback entries fail most often.

if EMA(5) > EMA(15) → trend = bullish → proceed to step 2
2

Pullback Entry — EMA(21)

Price must pull back to and bounce from the EMA(21). We don't chase — we wait for the market to come to us. The EMA(21) is one of the most widely followed moving averages among professional swing traders, acting as dynamic support in trending markets.

price touches EMA(21) + bounce confirmed → entry zone active
3

MACD Confirmation — Histogram Recovery

The MACD histogram must be recovering (turning positive). Momentum must be returning, not fading. This third confirmation prevents entering during prolonged pullbacks that turn into trend reversals.

MACD histogram > previous bar → momentum recovery confirmed → BUY
✓

All Three Align: Meridian Buys

When any one is absent: Meridian waits. Patience is a feature, not a bug.

Exit Methodology

Entries get the attention, but exits determine profitability. Meridian uses a dual-exit system designed to protect capital while allowing winners to develop.

ATR-Based Trailing Stop

Every position is protected by a trailing stop set at 1.5× ATR (Average True Range). This dynamically adapts to market volatility — tighter stops in quiet markets, wider stops when volatility is elevated. The stop locks in gains as positions move in your favor.

Signal-Based Exit

Sell signals trigger on MACD bearish divergence or EMA breakdown (EMA(5) crossing below EMA(15)). When the trend bias flips, Meridian exits regardless of profit level.

Take Profit Targets

Take profit is set at 8–20% depending on current market volatility (measured by ATR). Higher volatility = wider targets. Lower volatility = tighter, more conservative exits.

Risk Management

Meridian caps risk at 1.5% per position. Maximum 8 concurrent positions using risk parity position sizing — meaning each position contributes equally to portfolio risk regardless of the asset's volatility.

Combined with ATR-based stops and the triple confirmation entry, this creates a system that can sustain losing streaks without significant drawdown.

Key Parameters

  • Max risk per trade: 1.5%
  • Max concurrent positions: 8
  • Trailing stop: 1.5× ATR
  • Take profit: 8–20% (volatility-adjusted)
  • Typical hold time: 2–7 days
  • Trades per month: 20–40
  • Universe: Top-cap crypto pairs

When Meridian Waits

Momentum swing strategies perform best during trending markets. During sideways, range-bound conditions, Meridian reduces signal frequency naturally — the triple confirmation filter prevents most false entries.

This means some months will have fewer trades. That's by design. Not trading is sometimes the best trade.
